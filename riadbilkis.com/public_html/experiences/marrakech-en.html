Discover Marrakech

What to see in the medina and beyond, with distances from the riad and our tips to avoid the busiest hours.

Bab DoukkalaWalkable medinaHosts' tips

The must-sees

All reachable on foot or by petit taxi from the riad.

In the medina

  • Jemaa el-Fna square, 15 minutes' walk — best at the end of the day
  • Semmarine and dyers' souks
  • Koutoubia mosque and its gardens
  • Bahia palace and El Badi palace
  • Saadian tombs and Ben Youssef medersa
  • Marrakech museum and Dar el Bacha

Beyond the walls

  • Majorelle garden and Yves Saint Laurent museum
  • Menara gardens and the Palmeraie
  • Gueliz district for cafés and shops
  • Day trips: Ourika, Ouzoud, Essaouira, Agafay desert

Our tips

Best timing

Visit the monuments in the morning, rest at the riad in the afternoon, then walk down to Jemaa el-Fna at sunset.

Finding your way

The medina is very walkable. On arrival we give you an annotated map with districts, monuments and useful numbers.

Taxis

Petit taxis are cheap: ask for the meter or agree the fare before getting in. The riad is a few minutes from Bab Doukkala, where the taxi can drop you.

The souks

Take your time, compare, and bargain with a smile. We can point you to the craftsmen we know.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Jemaa el-Fna square?

Riad Bilkis is at Bab Doukkala, about 15 minutes' walk from Jemaa el-Fna square through the medina.

Can you visit Marrakech on foot?

Yes, the whole medina can be visited on foot. For the Majorelle garden, Gueliz or the Palmeraie, take a petit taxi.

What can you do on a day trip from Marrakech?

The Ourika valley, Ouzoud waterfalls, Essaouira or the Agafay desert all work well as day trips. We arrange transport and a guide.
