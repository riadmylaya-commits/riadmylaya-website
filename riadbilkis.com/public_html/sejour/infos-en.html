Gastronomy

The Moroccan breakfast

Every morning, a homemade Moroccan breakfast served on the terrace or in the patio, as you prefer. It is included in the room rate.

What you will find on the table

  • Homemade msemen and baghrir (Moroccan pancakes)
  • Fresh bread baked in a traditional oven
  • Homemade jams: fig, apricot, bitter orange
  • Pure honey from the Atlas
  • Olive oil and amlou
  • Freshly squeezed orange juice
  • Mint tea and coffee
  • Seasonal fruit

Times and flexibility

  • Served from 8:00 am to 10:30 am.
  • Before 7:30 am we prepare a takeaway breakfast for early departures.
  • After 10:30 am, breakfast is available for €6 per person.
  • Mrs. Khadija looks after breakfast and room cleaning, from 7:30 am to 3 pm.

Frequently asked questions

What time is breakfast served at Riad Bilkis?

The Moroccan breakfast is served from 8:00 am to 10:30 am and is included in the room rate. Before 7:30 am we prepare a takeaway breakfast. After 10:30 am, breakfast is available for €6 per person.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is possible from 1 pm and check-out is no later than 11 am. If your flight is very early or very late, let us know and we will arrange things.

Is there parking near the riad?

The riad is inside the medina, in Bab Doukkala. Parking is available nearby for €4 per 24-hour period; we send you the directions before you arrive.

Can I have my laundry done at the riad?

Yes. Leave your laundry in a bag next to the washing machine: the service costs €5 per load and is available from 9 am to 3 pm. Towels and bed linen are changed every two to three days depending on the length of your stay.

Does the riad arrange airport transfers?

Yes, with a private driver from and to Marrakech-Menara airport: €15 one way and €25 return. You can request it directly from this page.

A question before you come?

Write to us on WhatsApp or by email: we answer quickly, in English or French.
