How it works

  1. You choose a menu and a time; we confirm by email.
  2. Dinner is served in the patio or on the terrace, depending on the weather and your mood.
  3. Allergies, vegetarian diet, children: mention it in the form and the kitchen adapts.
  4. Payment is made on site, together with your stay.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dinner cost at Riad Bilkis?

Four menus are available, per person: main course €15, starter + main €20, main + dessert €20, full menu (starter, main, dessert) €25.

Do I need to book dinner in advance?

Yes, preferably 24 hours ahead: Mrs. Sinan shops at the market on the day to cook with fresh produce. For a last-minute request, write to us on WhatsApp.

Which dishes are served for dinner?

Homemade Moroccan dishes: tagines (chicken with preserved lemon, lamb with prunes, vegetables), couscous, chicken and almond pastilla, grilled skewers, Moroccan salads, pastries and mint tea.

Do you offer vegetarian or allergen-free menus?

Yes. Every menu exists in a vegetarian version, and we adapt dishes to allergies or special diets: just mention it in the booking form.

Is dinner only for guests staying at the riad?

Dinner is designed for guests staying at Riad Bilkis. The meal is served in the patio or on the terrace and is paid on site together with your stay.

A question before you come?

Write to us on WhatsApp or by email: we answer quickly, in English or French.
