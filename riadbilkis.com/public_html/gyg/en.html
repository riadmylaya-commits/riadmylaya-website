Group or private tour?

Both options depart from the Medina, minutes from the riad. Here is how to choose.

Group excursion

  • The best value for money, from €15 per person.
  • Fixed departure times and a minibus shared with other travellers.
  • Instant online booking, free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
  • Ideal for Agafay, Ourika, Ouzoud and Essaouira.
Book a group excursion

Private excursion

  • Driver and vehicle for you only, leaving at the time that suits you.
  • Itinerary adapted to your pace, photo stops on request.
  • The best choice with children or for a group of friends.
  • We also arrange your airport transfer.
