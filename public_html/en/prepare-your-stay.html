Arrival & luggage

Your room is available from 2 pm.

If you arrive earlier, you are welcome at the riad from 9 am to drop off your luggage.

If you arrive early in the morning, we can also serve you breakfast at €7 per person.

🥐 I would like breakfast on arrival — €7/person

Practical information

The essentials to know before you arrive.

🧾

City tax

€2.50 per person per night, payable at the riad. It is charged by the city of Marrakech and is not included in the room rate.

💵

Paying for extras

All extras and services you book (transfer, dinner, cooking class, excursions, hammam, extra breakfast) are paid on site in cash.

📍

Address of the riad

163 Derb Bounba, Arset Ihiri — Medina, Marrakech. The lane is pedestrian only, so your driver drops you at the nearest entrance.

Airport / station transfer

Airport from 20 € · Train station from 12 €

 ⌄

A driver waits for you at the airport or the train station and takes you straight to the riad. Fixed price, no haggling, no waiting.

  • A driver waits for you with a sign bearing your name; waiting time is included even if your flight is delayed.
  • Private air-conditioned vehicle, fixed price per vehicle whatever the amount of luggage.
  • Airport: 20 € (1 to 4 people), 25 € (5-6), 30 € (7), 35 € (8) · Train station: 12 € (1 to 2 people), 17 € (3-4), 20 € (5-6).
  • 5 € surcharge between 10 pm and 7 am for the airport, and for station departures between 3 am and 7:45 am.
  • Airport return trip: 5 € off the departure transfer, applied automatically.
