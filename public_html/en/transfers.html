Return airport transfer: €10 off

Book both the arrival and the departure with us: the €5 discount per trip is applied automatically, so €10 off in total.

Example: 1 to 4 people, return airport transfer = €30 instead of €40

Good to know

  • Airport: €5 surcharge per transfer between 10 pm and 7 am, on arrival as well as on departure.
  • Train station: €5 surcharge per transfer for departures between 3 am and 7:45 am.
  • The return discount applies to airport transfers only, not to station transfers.
  • All transfers are paid on site in cash.
  • To book, simply send us your date, your time and your flight or train number.
